Yours, start to finish.

Free
No account, no credits and no watermark. It's open source under Apache-2.0.
Private
Your media stays on your computer unless you choose a service that needs it. Every kind of reel is tested with the network blocked.
Quick
On an 8 GB Mac, 28 photos became a playing reel in 2.8 seconds, and a remix took under 30 milliseconds.
Editable
Every export comes with a project folder you can open and change.

Download it, open it.

For a Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 15 or later. The app carries everything it needs, and downloads one 93 MB piece the first time you open it.

Download for Mac Opening it the first time

Or install it with one line

Paste this into Terminal. It takes a few minutes and about 2 GB, and adds the ucut command.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/U-Cut/u-cut/main/install.sh | bash

Then type ucut to use it in your browser. On Linux, it runs in Docker, without sorting by scene.

Read the user guide Open the repo on GitHub

Or use the command line

ucut make ~/Pictures/Goa --vibe hype -o goa.mp4
ucut make ~/Pictures/Goa --like a-reel-i-love.mp4 -o goa.mp4
ucut demo recording.mov --name "Acme" -o demo.mp4
ucut write "a 20-second reel about my Goa trip, chill vibe" -o goa.mp4

Where it stands.

Works today

  • Reels cut to music, with remix, undo and a shot swap
  • Sorting into scenes, on a Mac
  • Matching the style of a reel you love
  • Demos from screen recordings
  • Reels from a sentence, with a local voice
  • Up to 20 people sharing one computer
  • Your assistant making reels, through MCP and the Claude Code plugin
  • Five photo styles with previews, sound effects and colour looks
  • A Mac app you download and open, with everything inside

Still rough

  • Sorting is right about two times in three on new places
  • Scripts come from fixed patterns unless you connect Claude
  • Captions and scripts are in English
  • The app isn't notarized yet, so macOS asks you to approve it once in System Settings

The plan is public, and templates, sound and code are all open to contributions.
