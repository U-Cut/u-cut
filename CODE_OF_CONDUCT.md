# Code of Conduct

U-Cut follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good faith, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment of any kind isn't tolerated.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/mayank29deo). Reports are kept private.
