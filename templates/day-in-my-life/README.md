# Day in my life

Your day in order, with the time each shot was taken.

The time sits in the top corner, as a phone shows it ("7:42 am"). Shots shorter than 0.8 seconds,
and photos without a capture time, go without one. The time is read on the Mac that makes the
reel, so it matches the clock the photos were taken by.

Made by the U-Cut contributors. Apache-2.0.
